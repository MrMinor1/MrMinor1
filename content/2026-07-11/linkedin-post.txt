"Which accounting software should we use?" is the wrong first question.

I've watched businesses run beautifully on QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks — and I've watched businesses drown in all three. After enough cleanups, the pattern is obvious: the software rarely fails. The system around it does.

That said, the tools do have real differences (2026 pricing, no affiliate anything):

• QuickBooks Online (from $38/mo): the network-effect choice. Every accountant, bookkeeper, and app integration speaks QBO. You pay for that ubiquity — literally, at every price increase.

• Xero (from $25/mo): unlimited users on every plan. If multiple people touch your books — ops manager, assistant, external bookkeeper — the math strongly favors it.

• FreshBooks (from $23/mo): the best invoicing and time-tracking experience for service businesses. Single-user by design; additional seats are $11/month.

But here's the question that actually predicts clean books: who reconciles, how often, and against what checklist? A $23 tool with a weekly 20-minute routine beats a $275 tool nobody opens until March.

All three now ship AI categorization. It's genuinely good — and it will confidently miscategorize edge cases at scale if the chart of accounts was set up wrong on day one. Automation amplifies your setup, in either direction.

So: pick the tool that fits how many hands touch your books. Then invest in the setup.
