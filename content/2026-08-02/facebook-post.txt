Four mistakes. Same business. Rank them by what they actually cost — most expensive first. 🏆

Same owner, $12,000 of tax owed, in all four:

1️⃣ Pays three months late
2️⃣ Files three months late
3️⃣ Never tracked 8,000 business miles
4️⃣ Buys $10,000 of gear in December "for the write-off"

Got your order? Here's the real one, cheapest to worst:

🥉 Paying three months late — $180. The failure-to-pay penalty is 0.5% per month. Annoying, plus interest, but survivable.

🥈 Filing three months late — $1,800. Failure-to-file is 5% per month, capped at 25%. Same delay, same balance, TEN TIMES the penalty.

That gap is the one almost everyone gets backwards. If money is tight and you can only do one thing: file. Filing without paying costs a tenth of paying without filing.

🥇 Untracked mileage — about $2,150. 8,000 miles at the 2026 rate of 72.5¢ is $5,800 of deduction you simply never claimed.

🏆 The December write-off spree — about $6,300 gone. You spent $10,000 to save roughly $3,700. A deduction discounts a purchase; it never pays for it.

Notice the pattern? The two that feel like tax mistakes are the cheapest. The two that feel like being smart or being busy cost the most.
