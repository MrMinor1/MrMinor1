The most under-claimed deduction we see? The home office. And the reason is almost always the same: "I heard it triggers audits." 🙄

Let's fix that today:

🏠 The two tests — the space must be used exclusively AND regularly for business. A dedicated desk corner qualifies; the kitchen table doesn't. (Self-employed and business owners only — W-2 remote employees can't claim it.)

🚐 The part contractors miss — you can work at client sites all day and STILL qualify, if home is where you handle admin, invoicing, and scheduling. That makes it your principal place of business.

🧮 Two ways to calculate — simplified ($5 per square foot, up to 300 sq ft = $1,500 max, zero receipts) or actual expenses (your office's percentage of rent/mortgage interest, utilities, insurance). We compare both every year — the winner changes as your costs change.

📸 The audit-proofing — measure the space, photograph it, keep the bills. Legitimate + documented = nothing to fear.
