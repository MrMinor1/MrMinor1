🏠 Your business can rent your home for up to 14 days a year — and you report $0 of that rent.

Swipe all 7 →

That's IRC §280A(g): use the home as a residence, rent it fewer than 15 days, and you don't report the income.

Fewer than 15 = 14 days max. Day 15 makes the whole year taxable.

Both sides work — the business deducts a reasonable rent, you exclude it.

But there's homework: a real business purpose, a fair market rate backed by written quotes, documentation, and a day count.

And the honest part nobody posts: if you're a sole prop or single-member LLC, this does NOTHING for you. No separate entity = paying yourself = nothing to exclude.
