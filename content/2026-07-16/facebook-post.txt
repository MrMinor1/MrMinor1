Unpopular opinion from actual tax advisors: a lot of you should NOT be paying a tax pro. 🙅

Here's the honest 4-question test:

1️⃣ Is all your income on a W-2 (plus maybe some bank interest)? TurboTax Free covers about 37% of filers; H&R Block Free covers about 52% — and Block includes state filing. Simple return? Keep your money.

2️⃣ Taking the standard deduction with no big life changes? Still DIY territory. Even itemizing, the Deluxe tiers run about $55–$69.

3️⃣ Side hustle with one 1099 and straightforward expenses? The self-employed tiers ($85–$119+) will walk you through it. Budget an afternoon.

4️⃣ Now the real question: are there DECISIONS in your year? Choosing an entity, adding payroll, working across states, buying equipment, profit climbing fast? Software can only report what already happened — it can't tell you what to do next. That's where professional advice pays for itself, usually many times over.

Pass all four? DIY with pride (honestly, Block is usually cheaper per tier). Stumble on #4? The tax return is the cheap part of what you need.
