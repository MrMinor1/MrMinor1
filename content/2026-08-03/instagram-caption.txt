2026 limits: employee deferral $24,500 ($32,500 at 50+, and $11,250 catch-up for ages 60–63). Combined cap $72,000.

Why the gap? A SEP-IRA has ONE lever — the employer percentage. A Solo 401(k) has TWO — that same percentage PLUS your own deferral. The SEP has no employee side at all.

Sole proprietor netting $100K, roughly:

The timing trap: you can now ESTABLISH a plan as late as your return's due date with extensions. But the deferral election generally has to be in place by Dec 31 — and the deferral is the bigger half.
