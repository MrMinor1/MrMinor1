Imagine a deduction where you don't have to buy anything, save any receipts, or spend a dime — you just get up to 20% of your business profit knocked off your taxable income. 💸

It exists. It's called the QBI (Qualified Business Income) deduction, and if you run a sole proprietorship, LLC, partnership, or S-Corp, you probably already get it — the question is whether you're getting ALL of it.

What's new in 2026:

📌 It's permanent now. Last year's tax law locked it in for good — no more expiration-date anxiety.

📌 There's a new floor: at least $400 of deduction if you have $1,000+ of active business income.

📌 The full 20% applies below roughly $200K of income (single) or $400K (joint). Above that, limits phase in — and service businesses feel the squeeze first.

Here's the part almost nobody tells you: you don't "claim" QBI — you SHAPE it. Your entity choice, your S-Corp salary, even your retirement contributions change the size of the number. Set your owner salary wrong and you can quietly shrink your own deduction by thousands.
