What if we told you the IRS publishes an actual cheat code — a rule that makes underpayment penalties completely optional? 🎮

It's called the safe harbor rule, and here's the whole thing:

🛡️ Option 1: Pay 100% of LAST year's total tax through this year's quarterly estimates. Doesn't matter if you earn triple this year — zero penalty. One number, set and forget. (High earners: it's 110% if your AGI topped $150K.)

🛡️ Option 2: Pay 90% of THIS year's tax as you go. The better play when income is down and you don't want to overpay based on a big prior year.

⚠️ The fine print: safe harbor eliminates the penalty, not the tax. If you earn more, the balance is still due in April — but you skip the ~6% penalty meter and keep your cash working for you until then.

Why this matters right now: the Q3 estimated payment is due September 15, and your safe harbor number takes about 10 minutes to calculate from last year's return.
