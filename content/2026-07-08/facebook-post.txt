Quick question, business owners: when's the last time you looked at your taxes... in July? 🗓️

If your answer is "never," you're not alone — most people think about taxes exactly once a year, in April, when it's too late to change anything.

But mid-year is when the real savings happen. Here's your July checkup:

💰 Check your estimated payments — the Q3 deadline is Sept 15. If income jumped this year, top up now and skip the penalty surprise.

🏦 Feed your retirement accounts — Solo 401(k) and SEP-IRA contributions can reach $72,000 in 2026, and every dollar can lower your taxable income.

📚 Catch up your bookkeeping — 6 months of clean books beats a January shoebox panic. Tools like QuickBooks Online or Xero can automate most of it.

🏢 Run the S-Corp math — if profits are climbing past ~$50K, a mid-year look at your entity structure can pay for itself.
