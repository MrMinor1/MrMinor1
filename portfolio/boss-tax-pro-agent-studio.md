# Boss Tax Pro AI Agent Studio

## Governed multi-agent research and media-production system

I designed and operated a versioned multi-agent AI system for Boss Tax Pro, a tax and bookkeeping education brand. The system separates current-law research, trend intelligence, script production, media operations, executive oversight, and Owner approval into distinct responsibilities.

The project demonstrates how I apply systems thinking to AI: define authority, control change, verify exact artifacts, preserve working baselines, document limitations, and prevent unreviewed output from reaching production.

## Business problem

The content workflow depended on current IRS research, calculations, bookkeeping context, competitor analysis, long-form scripts, short-form repurposing, production direction, and publication controls. A broad all-in-one AI workflow created unacceptable risks:

- unsupported or stale tax claims;
- calculations mixed with creative writing;
- unclear responsibility between agents;
- approved releases overwritten during repairs;
- packages, manifests, versions, and test results drifting out of sync;
- polished content passing structural checks while still lacking factual or editorial quality;
- unapproved evidence reaching public content.

## My solution

I built a governed agent program with separate responsibilities:

- **Agent-000:** executive oversight, routing, and governance review
- **Agent-001:** authoritative research, calculations, clarification, and evidence packets
- **Agent-002:** trend intelligence and opportunity analysis
- **Agent-003:** planned hybrid narrative and script-intelligence layer
- **Boss Tax Pro Media Engine:** content workflow orchestration
- **Boss competitive voice:** a separate creative standard for energetic, evidence-backed presentation

## Engineering controls

- Semantic versioning and immutable release baselines
- Genuine pytest and canonical CLI validation
- SHA-256 identity checks and detached manifests
- Exact-final-ZIP retesting after packaging
- Secret scans, archive inventory, clean extraction, and clean installation
- Explicit status vocabularies and stop conditions
- Five-file maximum agent handoffs
- Independent review separated from builder self-attestation
- Owner-controlled production and publication gates
- Strict separation between evidence and creative output

## Verified Agent-001 milestone

Agent-001 v1.0.2 completed:

- 35/35 genuine pytest tests
- 16/16 canonical CLI commands

Pilot progress as of July 30, 2026:

Agent-001 may perform internal research, source mapping, calculations, missing-fact analysis, and evidence preparation. It may not publish, provide final client-specific advice, sign off tax positions, certify other agents, or release downstream systems.
